# Reviewer and moderator onboarding

OpenLegal is seeking independent Moroccan legal reviewers, Arabic/French/English language reviewers, legal researchers, and community moderators. This page explains the volunteer process; it does not appoint anyone, verify qualifications, create a professional relationship, or authorize review status.

## Apply

Use the public [reviewer interest issue form](../.github/ISSUE_TEMPLATE/reviewer-interest.yml) in the public repository. GitHub issues and replies are public. Share only information you are comfortable publishing. Do not include identity documents, private contact details, client matters, privileged advice, confidential documents, or credentials. A short professional summary and a public profile or qualification link are enough for an initial expression of interest; maintainers may ask about scope or availability before assigning work.

## Volunteer roles

- **Legal reviewer:** assess a named Moroccan-law package or claim against its cited sources and defined scope. Record open questions, assumptions, and any limits on the review.
- **Language reviewer:** compare a named language version against the approved reference language and flag changes in meaning, obligations, defined terms, and omissions. Language review alone is not legal approval.
- **Research reviewer:** verify source identity, pinpoint references, amendments, and source-to-claim links. Source verification alone is not legal interpretation or template approval.
- **Community moderator:** help keep discussion respectful, on-topic, and free of sensitive information. Moderation does not grant legal or language review authority.

## Review process

1. A maintainer acknowledges the public expression of interest and clarifies the proposed role, scope, and availability.
2. Before an assignment, the project checks relevant experience and conflicts for that scope. A volunteer may decline or narrow an assignment.
3. The reviewer receives a packet for an exact repository revision, package version, and content digest. Do not review a moving branch or assume a previous review covers changed content.
4. The reviewer submits itemized findings and sources through the agreed project workflow. A maintainer records the outcome and any follow-up; the reviewer should not mark their own work approved.
5. Review status changes only after the repository's authorization, evidence, and signature checks pass. An issue, profile, qualification, or review packet is not itself approval.

All template packages and scanner rule interpretations currently remain unreviewed unless their specific records say otherwise. Legal review, language review, source verification, and community moderation are separate responsibilities. Reviewers should identify the exact scope and language they covered.

## Independence and rights

Disclose relevant conflicts and whether a review is independent. Do not claim independence where it does not apply. Do not submit client work or material you lack the right to share. Public contributions follow the repository's stated license scope, which is provisional and does not replace rights clearance.
