# Reviewer and moderator onboarding

This guide is for people interested in Moroccan legal review, Arabic/French/English language review, legal research, or community moderation.

## Register interest

Open [Issues → New issue](https://github.com/BaseWorkers/openlegal-morocco/issues/new) and choose **Join the reviewer or moderator program**. The form asks for role, scope, a short experience summary, optional public references, availability, and general conflicts or limits.

The form and all replies are public. Share only information you are comfortable publishing. Do not attach or post identity documents, CVs, private email or phone numbers, client or employer names, confidential work, credentials, or sensitive personal data. Public profile or publication links are optional. The project does not need identity documents for an initial expression of interest.

Submitting the form records interest only. It does not create employment, a professional relationship, repository access, reviewer authorization, or approval of any content. Public discussion is not confidential.

## Roles and boundaries

- **Legal reviewer:** proposes a defined area of Moroccan law and reviews an exact template version, its sources, and unresolved questions. Legal review is separate from language and community review.
- **Language reviewer:** proposes a language and direction, then checks that the reviewed version preserves meaning, obligations, defined terms, and legal effect. It does not establish legal accuracy.
- **Legal research reviewer:** checks primary sources, pinpoint references, amendments, commencement, and applicability questions. Research feedback does not by itself authorize `LEGAL_REVIEWED`.
- **Community moderator:** helps maintain respectful, useful public discussion and applies the Code of Conduct. Moderation never grants legal or language approval. Do not accept confidential security or conduct reports; the repository has no staffed private reporting channel yet.

## Screening and authorization

A project maintainer reviews the public role, scope, experience summary, optional public references, availability, and any stated conflicts. The maintainer may ask for public clarification or decline an application. No private documents should be requested through GitHub issues.

If a suitable scope is identified, the maintainer can propose a bounded assignment and confirm independence and conflicts before work starts. Repository write access is not required to review; findings can be submitted through the appropriate issue or pull request workflow.

Formal legal or language authorization begins only after qualification and role scope are verified, the reviewer agrees to the governance rules, and the reviewer is added to the appropriate registry through a reviewed pull request. Formal approvals must use the registered reviewer ID/key and bind to the exact content digest and scope. See [Governance](../GOVERNANCE.md) and [Reviewing](../REVIEWING.md).

The registries are currently empty. Until an authorized human reviewer is listed and an exact review record is verified, all 14 templates remain `DRAFT`. A moderator or maintainer cannot self-authorize legal or language approval.

## Review materials

After a successful Foundation CI run, download the `legal-language-review-packets` artifact and match the template ID, version, and content digest before beginning. See [the reviewing guide](../REVIEWING.md) for packet details and the required review record format.
