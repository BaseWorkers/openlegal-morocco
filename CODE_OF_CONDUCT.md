# Code of Conduct

OpenLegal is intended to be a respectful, useful space for people with different professional backgrounds, languages, and levels of legal knowledge.

## Expected conduct

- Treat other contributors with respect and discuss work on its merits.
- Make criticism specific, constructive, and free from harassment or personal attacks.
- Respect language and cultural differences; ask for clarification rather than assuming intent.
- Do not present personal views as legal advice, official guidance, or a project endorsement.
- Do not post confidential client information, personal data, credentials, or other sensitive material in public issues or pull requests.
- Disclose conflicts of interest that could materially affect a legal or policy review.

## Unacceptable conduct

Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, unwanted sexual attention, intimidation, and deliberate disruption are not acceptable. Publishing another person's private information without permission is prohibited.

## Reporting and enforcement

The project has a named technical maintainer and security-response owner; see [GOVERNANCE.md](GOVERNANCE.md). No conduct-specific responder or confidential conduct-reporting channel has been appointed. GitHub's private vulnerability-reporting route in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) is for security vulnerabilities, not conduct-only reports.

Do not post sensitive conduct details in public issues or pull requests. No confidential conduct-reporting route or response-time commitment is currently offered. This repository does not provide a hosted service.
