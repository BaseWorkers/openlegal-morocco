# Contracts — initial source note

**Status:** limited source-text locator; current-law status and legal interpretation unresolved.

## DOC Articles 230–231

WIPO Lex reproduces the French Code des obligations et des contrats through Law 53-05 and displays Articles 230 and 231. Article 230 addresses the binding effect and revocation of validly formed contractual obligations; Article 231 addresses good-faith performance and consequences supplied by law, usage, or equity according to the obligation's nature. The Ministry of Justice Directorate of Legislation also indexes corresponding text for these articles. An SGG-published [Commission Nationale de la Commande Publique opinion](https://www.sgg.gov.ma/Portals/0/commissions-marches/Avis_15.25_Fr.pdf) separately quotes Article 230 in a public-procurement dispute. That opinion is corroborating administrative guidance, not the promulgated DOC text or a general current-law determination. Its heading says 21 January 2025 while an opening recital states a 21 January 2024 deliberation date; the date discrepancy remains unresolved. This is a bounded text-level cross-check, not a current-law conclusion. See the proposition-level record in [`claims.json`](claims.json) and the [WIPO Lex reproduction](https://www.wipo.int/wipolex/en/legislation/details/2964).

A second SGG-published [CNCP opinion, n° 31/2020](https://www.sgg.gov.ma/Portals/0/commissions-marches/Avis_31.20_Fr.pdf?ver=2021-03-11-154523-357), states that any undertaking must be performed in good faith and then cites DOC Article 477 for the presumption of good faith. It does not say that its performance statement is Article 231 or quote Article 231.

The Ministry of Justice Adala portal now links a 282-page [Arabic DOC consolidation](https://adala.justice.gov.ma/api/uploads/2026/07/29/%D8%B8%D9%87%D9%8A%D8%B1%20%D8%A8%D9%85%D8%AB%D8%A7%D8%A8%D8%A9%20%D9%82%D8%A7%D9%86%D9%88%D9%86%20%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AA%D8%B2%D8%A7%D9%85%D8%A7%D8%AA%20%D9%88%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B9%D9%82%D9%88%D8%AF%20%D8%B5%D9%8A%D8%BA%D8%A9%20%D9%85%D8%AD%D9%8A%D9%86%D8%A9-1785316295256.pdf) whose cover is dated 16 July 2026. Its opening amendment list includes Law 041.25 and Law 40.24. The PDF's page 57 gives Articles 230–231; page 135 displays the amended Article 618-3 with a footnote to Law 041.25; page 141 displays Article 618-17 as repealed with a corresponding footnote. These are direct checks of the Ministry's consolidated text. The document is a consolidation, not the underlying Official Bulletin publications, and does not establish full amendment history, commencement, legal interpretation, or application. The Article 230–231 and Law 041.25 claims remain at `current_law_status: unresolved` pending Gazette reconciliation and qualified review. ## Bill 041.25 and Official Bulletin No. 7526

### Official Bulletin No. 7526 primary-source check

The SGG-hosted Arabic [Official Bulletin No. 7526](https://www.sgg.gov.ma/BO/AR/3111/2026/BO_7526_Ar.pdf) was opened and checked on 2026-10-08. Its running headers identify the issue date as 16 July 2026, resolving the secondary date conflict against the third-party issue index that says 27 July. The contents and Dahir heading identify Dahir 1.26.30, dated 3 July 2026, promulgating Law 041.25 (printed p. 4426). The published number is 041.25; one secondary article's internal 014.25 footnote mismatch is not supported by the primary text.

Law 041.25 Article 3 changes DOC Article 618-3 to require an authentic instrument for a preliminary sale contract for property under construction, under penalty of nullity. Article 5 repeals DOC Article 618-17 and says its implementing regulation remains in force until replaced (printed pp. 4426-4427). These are bounded source-text checks, not a conclusion about entry into force, interpretation, or complete current-law effect. The full amendment history and qualified legal review remain open. This real-estate-specific text does not establish a requirement for ordinary NDA, contractor, or services agreements. See the proposition-level records in [`claims.json`](claims.json).

The direct Ministry PDF request timed out, and the exact version/cutoff of that French file is not established. WIPO Lex states its reproduction is amended up to Law No. 53-05; that historical cutoff leaves later amendments to be checked. The Ministry also lists an Arabic consolidation titled through August 2024, which does not prove completeness through October 2026. These sources do not support a current-law conclusion.
