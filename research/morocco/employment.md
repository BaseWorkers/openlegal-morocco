## Source position

- SGG Official Bulletin n° 5210 contains the promulgation text of Law 65-99 relative to the Labour Code. The Ministry of Justice Adala portal also provides a French Code du travail PDF; direct inspection established that this copy is consolidated only through 26 October 2011.
- On 2026-10-08, the live Ministry social-law catalog directly displayed “Code du travail Nouveau” and linked to the Ministry PDF whose URL records an upload date of 2024-04-30. The 201-page PDF cover states “Version consolidée en date du 26 octobre 2011,” and its listed amendment history ends with a 2011 entry. “Nouveau” is a catalog label, not a consolidation date; the 2011 consolidation does not establish current law. Relevant provisions still require comparison against later Official Bulletins and qualified legal review.
- The Ministry PDF's indexed text for Articles 1 and 6 matches the corresponding provisions in SGG Official Bulletin n° 5210, printed page 603. This is a bounded text check of those provisions, not verification that the Ministry PDF is a complete consolidation or that those provisions remain current.
- The Ministry PDF's footnote 11 under Article 32 says paragraph 1 was abrogated by the sole article of Law 48-06 and cites SGG Official Bulletin n° 5522, p. 581. The SGG-hosted official French translation identifies issue n° 5522 as dated 3 May 2007; its Article unique on printed page 581 expressly lists Labour Code Article 32(1) and Article 256 among the provisions abrogated. It also records a transitional rule for conscripts already serving when the law took effect. The Ministry footnote instead gives 3 April 2007, so the date discrepancy is retained. This verifies what the accessed publication text says, not later amendments or current-law status; the Ministry PDF's consolidation cutoff remains unresolved.
- Claims for Articles 1 and 6 summarize the accessed promulgation text and are cross-referenced in [`claims.json`](claims.json). They are source-text checks only; current-law status is unresolved and no classification conclusion is made for a particular worker.
- SGG's official legislative portal listed Bill 032.26 as deposited on 15 May 2026 at the House of Councillors. The House of Councillors approved it on 16 June and the House of Representatives on 22 June 2026. The SGG Arabic [Official Bulletin No. 7526](https://www.sgg.gov.ma/BO/AR/3111/2026/BO_7526_Ar.pdf), printed p. 4422, publishes Dahir 1.26.27 dated 3 July 2026 promulgating Law 032.26 supplementing Labour Code Article 193. Article 2 states that, subject to its second paragraph, the law takes effect from publication; it gives companies providing guarding services up to nine months from publication to comply for employment contracts arising from guarding-services contracts concluded before publication. The filed bill had proposed twelve months for that transition, so the published nine-month text is the enacted wording on this point. The issue's running headers give 16 July 2026. These are bounded publication-text claims recorded in [`claims.json`](claims.json), not a complete Article 193 redline or a current-law/legal-application conclusion. The full Article 193 text, amendment history, affected parties, and application still require qualified review.
- Article 1's printed Article 193 passage is ellipsis-marked. Its visible excerpt includes a reference to guards other than those employed by guarding-services companies and to company workers, but ellipses interrupt the passage. The repository records only this bounded fragment in `labor-law-032-26-article-193-published-amendment-excerpt`; it does not reconstruct the omitted language or claim a complete amended Article 193. A clean consolidated text, amendment history, legal effect, affected parties, and current application remain open for qualified review.
- The Ministry of Justice Adala copy labels its French text as consolidated through 26 October 2011. Its pre-amendment Article 193 lists guardians among examples of work whose intermittent character may mean presence hours rather than effective working hours. A limited comparison with the 2026 Gazette amendment is recorded in `labor-law-032-26-article-193-compared-with-ministry-2011-copy`. Because that Ministry version predates Law 032.26 and the amendment itself is ellipsis-marked, this comparison does not establish the complete amended article or its legal effect; later amendments, scope, and application remain unresolved.
- Official Bulletin n° 6344 publishes Law 101-14 amending Dahir-Law 1-93-16, whose title describes employer-organized placements for holders of certain diplomas for formation-insertion. This is a scoped source lead, not a general authority for every student or workplace internship; current text and the fit of any placement remain unverified. The original Arabic general edition is Official Bulletin n° 6342, printed p. 1630; it records the then-stated 24-month non-renewable training cap in the replacement Article 4. Law 51.25 later states a different duration for the amended regime, so the historical text does not establish the period applicable to a particular contract.
- The SGG legislative portal and chamber records previously established Bill 51.25's filing and approvals. The SGG Arabic [Official Bulletin No. 7533](https://www.sgg.gov.ma/BO/AR/3111/2026/BO_7533_Ar.pdf), dated 10 August 2026, publishes Dahir 1.26.54 dated 28 July 2026 promulgating Law 51.25. Article 4 states that the law's provisions apply to training contracts concluded from publication; it also applies to contracts for trainees without diplomas concluded from 1 January 2025 through publication, referring to Finance Law 60.24 Article 14, while contracts for diploma-holding trainees concluded before publication remain subject to the prior Dahir-Law 1-93-16 text. The cited Finance Law text has been checked as a special-account expenditure provision. These are bounded text summaries, not conclusions about the complete current framework, regulatory implementation, benefit eligibility, or a particular internship; legal interpretation and full amendment history require review.
- On 2026-10-08, the SGG legislative portal's current page did not show Bill 51.25 in its listed projects; an SGG search-index result still surfaced a historical deposit listing for it. Treat that result as a historical procedural locator, not the current stage of the bill. Promulgation is established separately by Official Bulletin No. 7533. Neither the portal listing nor its absence establishes whether Law 51.25 requires or has received implementing instruments.
- Law 51.25's replacement Article 2 also caps the number of formation-insertion trainees hosted by an establishment by a percentage of its total employees, with that percentage to be set by a regulatory text. The Gazette therefore expressly identifies an implementing rule needed to calculate this cap. Focused SGG Official Bulletin searches on 8 October 2026 using Law 51.25 and Arabic/French terms for formation-insertion and the establishment-level employee percentage did not surface a separate implementing instrument in indexed results. This search is incomplete and is not evidence that no instrument exists; an unindexed or differently titled text may apply. Some results concerned COVID-era employee/trainee-retention percentages, which are distinct measures and are not treated as this cap. The applicable percentage and instrument remain unresolved; do not infer that the historical 60% hiring threshold under Law 101-14 is this separate trainee-cap percentage.
- Decree 2-91-517 is identified by a secondary Official Bulletin index as appearing in issue 4197. The ILO NATLEX reproduction states that its Articles 1–2 address the agreement model and an administrative visa and includes a historical model with terms such as an 18-month duration and a MAD 1,600 minimum. The SGG issue PDF was located but yielded no extractable text in this review, so these details remain a secondary text check, not a verified current requirement. The old model predates Law 101-14 and Law 51.25 and is not used in the internship draft.
- Official Bulletin No. 7399, printed p. 2390, publishes Decree 2.25.266 dated 24 April 2025. Article 3 provides for a ministerial order to specify wage elements, allowances, and employer-paid amounts outside the social-security contribution base and their exemption conditions/caps. Order 1314.25 expressly invokes Article 3(1), establishing the publication-text authority chain for item 24. This does not resolve amendment status, current effect, or how the training-specific contribution-base rule interacts with Law 51.25.
- Official Bulletin No. 7443 (Arabic, 29 September 2025) publishes Finance Minister Order No. 1314.25, dated 19 May 2025, implementing provisions of Decree 2.25.266 on the social-security contribution base. A corrigendum in Official Bulletin No. 7464, printed p. 9245, corrects part of item 24’s first trainee-qualification condition; it does not replace the separate duration paragraph. Annex item 24 describes conditions for excluding formation-insertion training compensation from that base, including ANAPEC registration, an endorsed training contract, monthly compensation between MAD 1,600 and MAD 6,000, and a 24-month duration extendible by 12 months in a stated hiring scenario. This is a source-text summary of a contribution-base condition, not a conclusion that the condition defines eligibility or the complete training-contract regime. Law 51.25, published on 10 August 2026 in Official Bulletin No. 7533, separately sets a 12-month non-renewable statutory training limit in its replacement Article 4. Article 4 applies the amended provisions to contracts concluded from publication, preserves the previous text for diploma-holder contracts concluded before publication, and separately covers specified non-degree contracts. The statutory-duration rule and contribution-base exemption condition address different questions; the difference in numbers alone does not establish a direct conflict or make the exemption condition permission to exceed the statutory limit. The effect of Order 1314.25 under the amended statute and the applicable contribution treatment remain for qualified legal review. Do not use either text to determine a particular placement.
- The cross-referenced [Finance Law 60.24](https://www.sgg.gov.ma/BO/AR/3111/2024/BO_7362_Ar.pdf), Article 14, is published in Official Bulletin No. 7362, printed p. 9800. From 1 January 2025 it adds to expenditure items of the special Treasury account named the Youth Employment Promotion Fund the State's assumption of specified benefits under Dahir-Law 1.93.16 for non-degree trainee training, for up to twelve months, and in case of permanent hiring. This describes public-account expenditure; it does not itself establish a trainee's individual entitlement, eligibility, or filing procedure. A separate source-text claim records this distinction. The broader interaction and application of these provisions remain for qualified review.

## Questions for authoritative research and counsel

- Find a current official consolidated Labour Code text and compare it provision by provision with amendments published after Bulletin 5210.
- Compare the published Law 032.26 text and Article 2 transition with the bill text and current Labour Code; Law 51.25's promulgation, publication, final text, and express Article 4 transitions are now recorded from Official Bulletin No. 7533, while their application and interaction with Law 60.24 remain open.
- Research employment-contract form and language, fixed/indefinite duration, probation, remuneration, working time, leave, termination, social protection, workplace safety, and mandatory information using current primary sources and exact pinpoints.
- Separately assemble the full current text and amendment history of Dahir-Law 1-93-16, including Law 101-14 and Law 51.25; verify implementing instruments and rules for education-linked student placements and vocational training. Do not assume all internships fall inside or outside any one regime.
- For the training regime, visually verify Decree 2-91-517 and its annex from Official Bulletin 4197; identify the current implementing text and percentage for Law 51.25's trainee-per-establishment cap; and verify other rules for education-linked student placements and vocational training. Do not infer that a historical agreement model or administrative visa remains current.
- Review employee confidentiality and intellectual-property terms against current Labour Code, copyright, industrial-property, and data-protection texts before drafting substantive allocation language. Compare relevant provisions in the SGG September 2026 consolidated Law 2-00 against underlying gazetted versions, then inspect Law 17-97 and its relevant amendment history.
- Identify which requirements vary by sector, worker category, company status, or collective agreement before making template fields or claims.
