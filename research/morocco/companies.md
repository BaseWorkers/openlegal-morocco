## Drafting and research safeguards

- Keep legal form, registration status, identifier data, and a signatory's authority as separate facts.
- Request identifiers only when applicable to the party and verified from its records or an authoritative source. Do not make RC, ICE, or IF universally mandatory template fields.
- Do not treat commercial-register filing authority as authority to bind an entity to a private contract. Verify the proposed signatory against current law, the entity's governing documents, appointments, delegations, and transaction-specific evidence.
- Do not state that one registration event or certificate proves every relevant identifier or signatory authority.
- The identified Adala texts are dated copies. Reconcile later amendments and relevant Official Bulletin publications before upgrading any proposition to current-law status.
- The six claim records for these checks are marked `source-text-checked` (or platform-description checked), `current_law_status: unresolved` for legal propositions, and `legal_review_status: not-reviewed`. They are research notes, not legal advice or generator instructions.
