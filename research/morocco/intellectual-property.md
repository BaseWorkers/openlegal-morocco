These are source-text summaries, not legal determinations or user-specific ownership conclusions. ### Law 013.26 text-level check

The Official Bulletin text shows Law 013.26 consists of three articles. Article 1 amends Law 2-00 Articles 1, 7, 10, 24(2), 43(1), 44, 59.1, 59.6, 60.2, 61, 61.2, and 64; Article 2 adds Article 1.7; Article 3 makes terminology substitutions in Articles 38, 51, and 65 and the Part II heading. Neither Articles 31 nor 35 appears in the Article 1 amendment list. This identifies only what Law 013.26 changes; it does not establish the complete current text or interpretation of Law 2-00.

The added Article 1.7 addresses commercial exploitation of works after their protection term expires. Its text assigns licensing and collection functions to the Moroccan Copyright Office, provides for a lump-sum basis under applicable legislative and regulatory texts, directs net collections after management costs to support cultural industries, and gives the Office an integrity/authenticity oversight role with authority to refuse harmful exploitation. This is a source-text paraphrase only; commencement, implementation, scope, and application remain unresolved.

## Legislative status and remaining verification

Official parliamentary records state that Bill 013.26 passed both chambers in July 2026 ([House of Representatives](#bill-013-26-adopted-by-both-chambers); [House of Councillors](#bill-013-26-adopted-by-both-chambers)). Its promulgation and publication are verified in Official Bulletin No. 7533 through Dahir 1.26.68. The SGG September 2026 consolidated edition has now been located and includes Law 013.26. Articles 31 and 35 have been checked against that edition as text-level propositions; their current-law status remains unresolved pending a complete amendment-history reconciliation. Law 013.26 has no separate commencement clause. Constitution Article 50 sets promulgation and publication deadlines; Article 6 affirms the obligation to publish legal norms and non-retroactivity. Neither provision states a default commencement date. The applicable general commencement rule remains unverified. Interpretation, application, reconciliation against underlying Gazette publications for other provisions, and human legal review also remain open.
