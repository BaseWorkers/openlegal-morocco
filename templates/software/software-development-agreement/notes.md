# Review notes

## Intended use

An issue-spotting discussion draft for a client commissioning custom software work. It is not a final form, legal advice, or a claim that any term is enforceable or current under Moroccan law. The `MA` tag is for discovery only. Do not sign or use for generation.

## Provenance and limits

- General-Legal was consulted only for comparative issue spotting. No wording, clause text, branding, or structure was copied or adapted.
- The Adala Code des obligations et des contrats portal entry and OMPIC-hosted Law 23-13 are research locators. Neither is a consolidated statement of current law; this draft asserts no article-level rule.
- The draft is independently written as a checklist of subjects for the parties and qualified counsel. The source list does not validate its terms.

## Required legal review

- Confirm identity, capacity, authority, contract formation, execution method, language, governing law, forum, and any mandatory rules from current primary texts.
- Define the project scope, specifications, milestones, dependencies, testing, acceptance, change control, delay handling, and completion evidence in an attached SOW.
- Decide ownership and licensing for pre-existing materials, deliverables, source code, tools, third-party components, open-source dependencies, and documentation; verify any formalities against current IP law.
- Complete confidentiality, security, personal-data roles and instructions, subcontracting, warranties, remedies, liability, termination, transition, and support terms.
- Check third-party and open-source license compatibility and inventory all dependencies before delivery.

## Required language review

EN/FR/AR are parallel working drafts only. A qualified reviewer must verify legal-intent parity, Moroccan terminology, technical vocabulary, numbering, punctuation, and RTL rendering. No language has been reviewed.
