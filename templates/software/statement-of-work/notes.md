# Review notes

## Intended use

A project-scoping attachment discussion draft for a software/services engagement. It is not a standalone contract, legal advice, or a statement that any commercial term is enforceable. Use with a separately reviewed framework agreement where appropriate; the `MA` tag is for discovery only.

## Provenance and limits

- General-Legal was consulted only for comparative issue spotting. No wording, clause text, branding, or structure was copied or adapted.
- The Adala Code des obligations et des contrats portal entry is an official research locator, not a consolidated current-law source. This draft makes no article-level or mandatory-law claim.
- The draft independently organizes project facts for parties and their advisers to complete. It does not determine acceptance, payment, IP, or remedy effects.

## Required legal review

- Confirm the governing master agreement, document precedence, authority, execution, and whether the SOW becomes binding and by what process.
- Align scope, deliverables, schedule, dependencies, review and acceptance steps, change handling, price, expenses, and payment events with the master agreement.
- Identify IP deliverables and dependencies, data access, security controls, third-party software, and any support or hosting responsibilities.
- Review dates and commercial triggers for mandatory rules and the actual parties and transaction.

## Required language review

EN/FR/AR are structurally parallel working drafts only. Review meaning parity, technical terms, dates and number formats, Moroccan business terminology, and Arabic RTL presentation.
