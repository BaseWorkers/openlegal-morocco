# Review notes

## Intended use

A discussion scaffold for accurately describing one service’s cookies and similar technologies. It does not decide which technologies are legally regulated or which user-control mechanism is required. The notice is not ready to publish until technical facts, vendor locations, user controls, and current-law questions are resolved.

## Provenance and limits

- Official source records include the promulgated Loi 09-08, Decree 2-09-165, and CNDP procedure guidance accessed on 2026-10-08.
- The source registry now records the CNDP's website-compliance guide and Deliberation D-939-2025 (dated 28 November 2025). The decision states scoped cookie-data rules on simplified declaration, purposes, consent, six-month retention, and transfers; the guide and decision use different cookie-consent wording. Their legal relationship, current effect, and application to a particular technology remain unresolved in this project.
- Existing Loi 09-08 Article 1–5 claims remain text-level, current-law-unresolved, and unreviewed. The CNDP procedure page is official regulator guidance but does not establish the law’s application to a particular technology.
- `sources.yaml` links selected research claim IDs to the corresponding source records. This makes the relationship auditable; it does not imply that a claim is current, legally reviewed, or applicable to this service.
- The draft does not assert that all cookies are personal data, or select a consent/banner/opt-in/opt-out mechanism or retention period. Do not use it without checking actual technologies against the CNDP decision and obtaining qualified review.
- The template contains no copied foreign-law wording. Comparative references are for issue spotting only.

## Required completion and review

- Inventory all technologies across production websites and apps, including domains, scripts, tags, SDKs, pixels, storage, embedded media, analytics, advertising, support tools, and vendor access.
- Record provider, purpose, category, first/third-party status, data collected, identifiers, storage duration, user control, and any locations or transfers based on actual technical testing and vendor documentation.
- Test current desktop/mobile controls, preference persistence, rejection, revocation, browser settings, and changes in deployed tags.
- Have qualified Moroccan counsel verify current applicable law and whether information, consent, other choices, CNDP steps, or a separate privacy notice are relevant to the actual setup. Do not invent procedures or deadlines.
- Have a qualified language reviewer verify legal intent parity and Moroccan terminology in EN/FR/AR, including Arabic RTL rendering.
