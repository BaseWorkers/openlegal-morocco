# Review notes

## Intended use and role caution

A discussion draft for a relationship where one party proposes processing personal data for another. It is not legal advice or a determination of legal roles. The parties’ labels do not decide their roles; document the actual purposes, decisions, access, services, and instructions. The package is not for signature or generation.

## Research source boundaries

- `law-09-08-processor-security-contract` summarizes Article 23(1)-(4) of the accessed promulgation text. `law-09-08-cross-border-transfer-articles-43-44` summarizes Articles 43–44. Both are marked current-law unresolved and not legally reviewed.
- `sources.yaml` maps both propositions to their cited CNDP and Official Bulletin records; these links do not indicate current-law verification or legal approval.
- The source set includes the CNDP-hosted Loi 09-08 text, Official Bulletin n° 5714, the CNDP-hosted implementing decree, and CNDP procedure guidance. The decree and guidance do not establish that any procedure applies to a specific arrangement.
- No deadline, filing requirement, country adequacy result, CNDP authorization, or approval is asserted. No current consolidated text or complete amendment history has been verified.
- Clause structures are independently prepared; no foreign or General-Legal wording is copied or adapted.

## Required legal review

- Confirm actual parties, roles, territorial scope, purposes, decisions, data access, and whether the arrangement fits the source-text terminology.
- Verify current Article 23 text, related rules, and any implementation or CNDP process applicable to the facts.
- Complete the processing schedule and documented instructions, data categories, sensitive data, duration, and service details.
- Review technical and organizational safeguards, confidentiality, subprocessor authorization and change process, cross-border access, retention, assistance, incident communication, audit, return/deletion, liability, and precedence.
- Determine whether an incident notification period is a contractual choice and verify any separate statutory duties; this template’s placeholder is not a legal deadline.
- Confirm transfers against current law, the exact destination, all remote access and vendors, and applicable CNDP guidance. Do not infer permission from this template.
- Obtain qualified Moroccan legal review of the completed agreement and attachments.
