# Review notes

## Intended use

A customizable privacy-notice discussion draft for one named website, product, or service. It is not ready to publish because actual organization and processing facts are required. It is not legal advice, does not guarantee compliance, and has not been reviewed by Moroccan counsel or language reviewers. The MA tag supports discovery only.

## Legal source and claim boundaries

- The source package points to the promulgated Loi 09-08, its original Official Bulletin, Decree 2-09-165, and current CNDP procedure guidance accessed 2026-10-08.
- The Ministry of Justice's Adala portal also hosts a PDF copy of Loi 09-08. It is recorded as a locator only: its full text has not been compared with the Official Bulletin, and the upload date/path does not establish that it is consolidated or current.
- The source package now also points to CNDP's [`Conformité des sites web`](../../../sources/registry.yaml) guidance as a research reference. Its broad direction on notification and consent is recorded in `research/morocco/claims.json`. Its consent direction is broader in wording than Article 4's general rule with enumerated necessity exceptions in the 2009 promulgation text; that textual difference does not itself prove legal inconsistency or current applicability. Do not convert either text into a universal template requirement.
- The research registry contains selected Article 1–5 text-level propositions. Each remains current-law unresolved and not legally reviewed. In particular, [`law-09-08-collection-information`](../../../research/morocco/claims.json) is a paraphrase of Article 5(1), not a complete or current notice checklist.
- `sources.yaml` maps the Article 5(1) proposition to its cited CNDP and Official Bulletin records. This is traceability metadata, not a legal approval or current-law determination.
- Selected text checks for Loi 09-08 Articles 7–10 now cover access, correction, objection, and certain direct-marketing channels. They remain current-law unresolved and do not establish what rights, procedures, or channels apply to this service. Article 10's conditional email-marketing passage must not be converted into a blanket permission; verify the actual collection context, products/services, notices, and objection handling with counsel.
- The CNDP process page is regulator guidance. Its listed categories and steps do not establish what applies to a particular controller, processing activity, data category, destination, or vendor. No deadline, filing, authorization, exemption, or approval statement appears as an asserted obligation in this template.
- No exact current consolidated text or complete amendment history has been verified. The template therefore does not state that any listed section or field is legally required.
- Two separate legislative proposals concerning Loi 09-08 are tracked as procedural leads: the PJD group's February 2024 proposal and the Mouvement group proposal registered 15 October 2024 to amend Articles 1 and 43. Neither record establishes an enacted amendment or current-law effect; do not treat proposed language as operative law.
- No General-Legal wording was copied or adapted. The template was independently structured around factual transparency and counsel review.

## Required completion and legal review

- Identify the actual responsible organization, service, contact channel, and the scope of this notice.
- Inventory data collected directly and indirectly, sources, purposes, recipients, processors, integrations, access locations, retention, and cookie/similar technologies. Match every statement to production systems and vendor contracts.
- Determine applicable legal roles and grounds for each purpose. Do not default to consent or transplant GDPR concepts.
- Verify the current law and applicable rights, information rules, exceptions, retention, security, transfer rules, procedures, and any required separate documents against primary texts and current CNDP guidance.
- Check cross-border access and vendor locations on actual data flows; do not say “no transfers” until verified.
- Confirm request intake and response workflows can be operated as written. Do not invent rights, deadlines, identity requirements, or outcomes.
- Obtain qualified Moroccan legal review of the complete notice and all language versions before publication.

## Required language review

EN/FR/AR are structurally parallel work drafts only. A qualified reviewer must assess legal meaning parity, Moroccan terminology, Arabic RTL, numbering, and punctuation. Structural parity does not establish equivalent meaning.
