# Review notes

## Intended use

A discussion outline only. It does not determine whether an offer is binding, what terms are mandatory, or whether an employment relationship is formed. Counsel and HR must confirm every substantive term, the legal effect and duration of any offer, any response deadline, and applicable current law before use. The source records are research leads, not proof of current requirements. All counsel-controlled variables are blocked from generation.

## Research and translation limits

The sources are research locators and dated text checks only. Current consolidated law, amendments, interpretation, and application to the actual facts are unresolved. EN/FR/AR are unreviewed working translations; equivalent meaning, terminology, numbering, and Arabic RTL require qualified language review. No wording was copied from General-Legal or another template source.
