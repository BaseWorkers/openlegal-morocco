# Review notes

## Intended use

An issue-spotting discussion draft for a proposed workplace learning placement. It does not classify the trainee, establish that the arrangement is an internship rather than employment, or replace a special statutory form. The word “internship” is not a legal conclusion.

## Research limits

- Official Bulletin n° 6344 publishes Law 101-14 amending Dahir-Law 1-93-16. The title describes employer-organized placements for holders of certain diplomas for formation-insertion. This is a specific source lead, not a general rule for all student or workplace placements.
- Official Bulletin No. 6342 (Arabic original; French translation in No. 6344) publishes Law 101.14. Its replacement Article 4 stated a 24-month non-renewable duration, with a narrow repeat-training provision within the same aggregate cap. Law 51.25 later changes the regime and sets a different duration. The historical provision is not used as a contract term; current application and transitions remain unresolved.
- Decree 2-91-517 is listed as implementing Dahir-Law 1-93-16. The ILO NATLEX reproduction says its Articles 1–2 concern the agreement model and administrative visa and includes an old annex model with historical terms. The SGG Official Bulletin issue PDF was located but its text could not be extracted in this review; neither the reproduction nor the old annex establishes current requirements after Law 101-14 and Law 51.25. No wording or field from that annex is adopted here.
- Official Bulletin No. 7533 publishes Dahir 1.26.54 and Law 51.25 amending Dahir-Law 1-93-16. Article 4 states the transition terms by training-contract date and whether the trainee holds a diploma. Finance Law 60.24 Article 14 is cross-referenced for specified non-degree contracts; its official text concerns State-assumed benefit expenditure through a special Treasury account and is not by itself proof of a trainee's direct entitlement.
- Official Bulletin No. 7399 publishes Decree 2.25.266. Article 3 provides for a ministerial order specifying exclusions from the CNSS contribution base and their exemption conditions/caps; Order 1314.25 cites Article 3(1). This establishes the published source chain only, not the current application of the training-specific rule.
- Official Bulletin No. 7443 publishes Order 1314.25, whose annex item 24 states a 24-month duration (with a possible 12-month extension in a stated hiring scenario) as one condition for excluding compensation from the social-security contribution base. Official Bulletin No. 7464 corrects part of item 24’s first qualification condition; the corrigendum does not replace its duration paragraph. Law 51.25, published on 10 August 2026, separately states a 12-month non-renewable statutory training limit for contracts governed by the amended regime and sets contract-date and trainee-category transitions. These texts address different questions; the numerical difference alone does not establish a direct conflict, and the Order 1314.25 exemption condition is not treated here as permission to exceed the statutory duration. The order's effect under the amended statute, applicable contribution treatment, and CNSS implementation remain for qualified review; this draft does not encode either duration as a term.
- The Labour Code source set is not a verified current consolidated text. The legal category and applicable rules for this specific placement are unresolved.
- No eligibility, duration, stipend, insurance, social-protection, recruitment, school-approval, or reporting requirement is asserted here.
- No wording was copied from General-Legal or another template source.

## Required legal and institutional review

Before use, qualified Moroccan counsel and the relevant school or training institution must identify the applicable regime and current official forms, determine whether the arrangement is covered by a special formation-insertion scheme or another framework, and review activities, supervision, schedule, compensation, safety, insurance, data, intellectual property, evaluation, and ending terms. Reconcile Law 51.25 and its transition with the full current text of Dahir-Law 1-93-16, Finance Law 60.24, implementing instruments, and the facts of the proposed placement.
