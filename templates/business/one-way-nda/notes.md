# Review notes

## Intended use

Structured one-way confidentiality discussion draft for a business disclosure. The Discloser is the only disclosing party under this draft. Jurisdiction metadata is for project discovery only; it is not a conclusion that Moroccan law governs or validates any clause. Generator use is disabled.

## Provenance and drafting method

- `general-legal-templates` was consulted only for comparative issue spotting and repository/license context. No wording was copied or adapted.
- The Adala DOC entry is only a research locator for counsel to verify the current legal framework. It does not establish a proposition about current Moroccan law, and no such proposition is stated or derived in the template text.
- The clauses are independently drafted issue prompts. Their legal effect, completeness, enforceability, and suitability have not been reviewed.

## Required legal review questions

- Confirm current Moroccan law and conflict-of-laws treatment for the proposed governing-law and dispute selections.
- Review definition, marking/identification, oral disclosures, prior disclosures, exclusions, burdens of proof, and disclosures to affiliates or advisers.
- Review treatment of personal data, professional secrecy, regulated information, trade secrets, source code, and legally compelled disclosures.
- Review permitted security standard, recipient responsibility, duration, survival, return/deletion, backup retention, and non-waivable duties.
- Decide whether remedies, liability limits, warranties, intellectual property, assignment, severability, counterparts, electronic execution, and language priority need terms.
- Confirm corporate identity and signatory authority fields and appropriate notice mechanics.
- Confirm the one-way allocation is appropriate; if both parties disclose information, use a separately reviewed mutual agreement.

## Required language review

English, French, and Arabic are structurally parallel working drafts only. No qualified translator has verified terminology, legal meaning parity, Arabic directionality/layout, or whether one language should control. Treat all three as unreviewed.

## Limitations

Do not use as a signed agreement or represent this package as legally reviewed. A counsel review must cover the exact content digest and version before legal-review status can change.
