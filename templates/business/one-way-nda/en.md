This One-Way Non-Disclosure Agreement (the “Agreement”) is entered into on {{effective_date}} by and between **{{discloser_name}}** ({{discloser_form}}), with an address for notices at {{discloser_address}}, represented for signature by {{discloser_signatory}} (the “Discloser”), and **{{recipient_name}}** ({{recipient_form}}), with an address for notices at {{recipient_address}}, represented for signature by {{recipient_signatory}} (the “Recipient”).

<!-- section: purpose -->
## 1. Purpose

The Discloser may provide information to the Recipient solely so the Recipient can evaluate or carry out this purpose: **{{purpose}}** (the “Purpose”). The Recipient may not use the Discloser’s Confidential Information for another purpose without written permission.

<!-- section: definition -->
## 2. Confidential Information

“Confidential Information” means information that the Discloser or someone acting for it makes available to the Recipient for the Purpose, in any form, that is marked confidential or that reasonably should be understood to be confidential in the circumstances. It includes copies and notes that contain or reflect that information.

The Parties should identify during legal review whether this definition needs specific treatment for oral disclosures, personal data, regulated information, source code, or information disclosed before {{effective_date}}.

<!-- section: exclusions -->
## 3. Exclusions

Subject to applicable law and evidence available to the Recipient, Confidential Information does not include information the Recipient can show:

- was publicly available without breach of a duty owed to the Discloser;
- was already lawfully known to it without a confidentiality restriction;
- was received lawfully from another source that, to its knowledge, was not under a duty to keep it confidential; or
- was independently developed without use of or reference to Confidential Information.

Counsel should review the scope, proof requirements, and any exclusions required by applicable law.

<!-- section: handling -->
## 4. Use, protection, and permitted access

The Recipient may use Confidential Information only for the Purpose. It will protect it using reasonable care and at least the care it uses for its own similar information. It may give access only to its personnel and professional advisers who need the information for the Purpose and are bound by confidentiality duties. The Recipient is responsible under this draft for their handling of the information.

<!-- section: compelled-disclosure -->
## 5. Required disclosure

If the Recipient is required by a binding legal process to disclose Confidential Information, it may disclose only the portion it is advised is required. Where legally permitted, it will give the Discloser prompt notice and reasonable cooperation, at the Discloser’s cost, to seek confidential treatment. Counsel should confirm this clause against applicable mandatory rules and process.

<!-- section: duration -->
## 6. Term and continuing duties

This Agreement begins on {{effective_date}} and allows disclosures for {{agreement_term}}. The confidentiality duties continue for {{confidentiality_period}} after the Agreement ends. The Parties must obtain legal advice on appropriate periods, any different treatment by information type, and any duties that cannot be limited by contract.

<!-- section: return-deletion -->
## 7. Return or deletion

On written request or when the Agreement ends, the Recipient will stop using and, as the Discloser directs, return or delete Confidential Information under its control. It may retain copies only to the extent required by law or held in routine backups that are not reasonably accessible for ordinary use; retained copies remain subject to the confidentiality duties. Counsel should confirm whether these exceptions and deletion steps are appropriate.

<!-- section: rights-no-commitment -->
## 8. Rights and no further commitment

Confidential Information remains with the Discloser. This Agreement grants no licence or other right except the limited right to use information for the Purpose. Neither Party is required by this Agreement to proceed with a transaction or make any disclosure. Each Party should obtain advice about any warranties, remedies, or other terms it needs; this draft does not resolve them.
