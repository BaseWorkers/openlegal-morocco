This Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement (the “Agreement”) is entered into on {{effective_date}} by and between:

1. **{{party_a_name}}** ({{party_a_form}}), with an address for notices at {{party_a_address}}, represented for signature by {{party_a_signatory}} (“Party A”); and
2. **{{party_b_name}}** ({{party_b_form}}), with an address for notices at {{party_b_address}}, represented for signature by {{party_b_signatory}} (“Party B”).

Each is a “Party” and together they are the “Parties.”

<!-- section: purpose -->
## 1. Purpose

The Parties may exchange information solely to evaluate or carry out this purpose: **{{purpose}}** (the “Purpose”). Neither Party may use the other Party’s Confidential Information for another purpose without written permission.

<!-- section: definition -->
## 2. Confidential Information

“Confidential Information” means information that a Party or someone acting for it (“Disclosing Party”) makes available to the other Party (“Receiving Party”) for the Purpose, in any form, that is marked confidential or that reasonably should be understood to be confidential in the circumstances. It includes copies and notes that contain or reflect that information.

The Parties should identify during legal review whether this definition needs specific treatment for oral disclosures, personal data, regulated information, source code, or information disclosed before {{effective_date}}.

<!-- section: exclusions -->
## 3. Exclusions

Subject to applicable law and evidence available to the Receiving Party, Confidential Information does not include information the Receiving Party can show:

- was publicly available without breach of a duty owed to the Disclosing Party;
- was already lawfully known to it without a confidentiality restriction;
- was received lawfully from another source that, to its knowledge, was not under a duty to keep it confidential; or
- was independently developed without use of or reference to the Confidential Information.

The Parties should have counsel review the scope, proof requirements, and any exclusions required by applicable law.

<!-- section: handling -->
## 4. Use, protection, and permitted access

The Receiving Party may use Confidential Information only for the Purpose. It will protect it using reasonable care and at least the care it uses for its own similar information. It may give access only to its personnel and professional advisers who need the information for the Purpose and are bound by confidentiality duties. The Receiving Party is responsible under this draft for their handling of the information.

<!-- section: compelled-disclosure -->
## 5. Required disclosure

If the Receiving Party is required by a binding legal process to disclose Confidential Information, it may disclose only the portion it is advised is required. Where legally permitted, it will give the Disclosing Party prompt notice and reasonable cooperation, at the Disclosing Party’s cost, to seek confidential treatment. Counsel should confirm this clause against applicable mandatory rules and process.

<!-- section: duration -->
## 6. Term and continuing duties

This Agreement begins on {{effective_date}} and allows disclosures for {{agreement_term}}. The confidentiality duties continue for {{confidentiality_period}} after the Agreement ends. The Parties must obtain legal advice on appropriate periods, any different treatment by information type, and any duties that cannot be limited by contract.

<!-- section: return-deletion -->
## 7. Return or deletion

On written request or when the Agreement ends, the Receiving Party will stop using and, as the Disclosing Party directs, return or delete Confidential Information under its control. It may retain copies only to the extent required by law or held in routine backups that are not reasonably accessible for ordinary use; retained copies remain subject to the confidentiality duties. Counsel should confirm whether these exceptions and deletion steps are appropriate.

<!-- section: rights-no-commitment -->
## 8. Rights and no further commitment

The Disclosing Party retains the rights and interests it holds in its Confidential Information. Disclosure does not transfer ownership of that information. This Agreement grants no licence or other right except the limited right to use information for the Purpose. Neither Party is required by this Agreement to proceed with a transaction or make any disclosure. Each Party should obtain advice about any warranties, remedies, or other terms it needs; this draft does not resolve them.
