# Review notes

## Intended use

A framework discussion draft to help business parties and Moroccan counsel structure proposed recurring or multi-stage services. It is not a signed-form contract or legal advice. The MA tag is for discovery only. No current Moroccan law proposition is asserted in the body.

## Provenance and drafting method

- `general-legal-templates` was consulted for comparative issue spotting and the source repository’s stated CC0 status. No wording, branding, repository structure, or clause text was copied or adapted.
- `adala-portal-code-obligations` is included only as an official locator for counsel to identify and verify potentially relevant current law. The locator does not itself support a proposition, and no DOC article or legal rule is claimed in this package.
- Terms are independently drafted prompts for discussion. Their legal effect, completeness, suitability, and interaction with mandatory rules have not been reviewed.

## Required legal review

- Confirm the parties’ legal identities, capacity, authority, and whether this framework plus separate SOWs fits the transaction.
- Review contract formation, amendments, electronic execution, language priority, governing law, jurisdiction, dispute resolution, and any mandatory formalities under current law.
- Complete service scope, acceptance, service levels, client dependencies, change control, fees, currency, invoices, payment timing, disputed invoices, late payment, expenses, taxes, and any mandatory requirements.
- Draft complete confidentiality, background IP, deliverables, third-party/open-source material, data-processing, security, warranties, indemnities, liability, remedies, force-majeure, termination, transition, and precedence terms.
- Determine whether separate NDA, SOW, data, IP, or other instruments are needed and ensure precedence is coherent.
- Verify applicable current primary legal sources and do not rely on the Adala portal index alone for a substantive proposition.

## Required language review

EN/FR/AR are structurally parallel working drafts only. A qualified reviewer must verify meaning parity, Moroccan legal and business terminology, numbering and punctuation, Arabic RTL layout, and whether a controlling-language term is appropriate. Structural parity is not semantic review.
