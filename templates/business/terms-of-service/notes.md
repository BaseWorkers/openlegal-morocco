## Required legal review

- Determine the service’s actual audience and whether it is B2B, B2C, or mixed; verify the current consumer definition and applicability.
- Retrieve and reconcile current Law 31-08, including later amendment status and interpretation of Article 202, its implementing texts, and any pertinent distance-sales rules.
- Verify the current Law 53-05 and Law 43-20 framework, including later Official Bulletin materials, against the actual offer, order, confirmation, correction, and record flow.
- Review service descriptions, identity disclosures, eligibility, account rules, prices, taxes, renewals, payments, cancellation/refunds, user content, third parties, privacy, changes, suspension, warranties, liability, and dispute terms for this product.
- Check every variable against actual live product behavior and contracts; do not promise a feature or process the operator cannot perform.
- Obtain qualified Moroccan legal and language review before publication or generator enablement.
