# Independent Contractor Agreement — discussion draft

**Not for signature or generator use.** This unreviewed discussion draft does not determine whether a person is legally an employee, worker, or independent service provider. A contract label or the parties’ stated intention cannot resolve classification. The actual working relationship, current Moroccan law, and relevant evidence require review by qualified Moroccan counsel. The Labour Code materials listed for this package include an original promulgation text, a portal copy, and a proposed amendment; they do not establish the current rules or a complete classification test. No classification conclusion is made here.

This draft is for issue spotting only. It is not legal advice, and no term is represented as valid, complete, enforceable, or suitable under Moroccan law. Do not sign or rely on it before legal and language review.
