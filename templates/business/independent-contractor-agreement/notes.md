# Review notes

## Intended use

A structured discussion draft to help the parties and Moroccan counsel identify terms and facts for a proposed services engagement. It is not a signed-form contract, legal advice, a classification determination, or a generator-enabled template. The Morocco jurisdiction tag supports discovery only.

## Classification and current-law limits

- The Labour Code Article 6 material in the source set records the promulgated text of an employment definition but is not a complete test for every relationship and does not establish current consolidated law.
- The Adala portal copy has not been audited for its consolidation cutoff or amendment history.
- Bill 032.26 is a proposal, not proof of enactment or commencement; its legislative status remains unresolved in project research.
- The DOC portal entry is a research locator and supports no substantive proposition in this package.
- No claim is made about worker classification, tax, social-protection, registration, invoicing, notice, IP ownership, electronic execution, or any CNDP process. Counsel must verify the law and facts that apply to the parties and proposed work.
- A written label or intention does not decide legal status. Counsel should assess the real working arrangement, including direction/control, schedule and place, tools, integration, exclusivity, substitution, financial arrangements, and other relevant evidence without treating this non-exhaustive list as a legal test.

## Provenance and drafting method

- The SGG Official Bulletin and Adala Labour Code copy are listed as research materials, not as proof of current law. Article 6 wording alone is not used to conclude that a person is an independent contractor.
- The proposed amendment is tracked as a legislative-status lead only. Do not describe proposed wording as enacted law.
- The Adala DOC portal is only a locator; no DOC-based claim is made.
- No wording was copied from General-Legal or another template source. The draft is independently structured as an issue-spotting document.

## Required legal review

- Determine whether and how current Moroccan law classifies the proposed relationship based on actual facts, and identify mandatory rules and rights that cannot be displaced by contract.
- Confirm the parties' capacity, authority, and any required identifying details.
- Review scope, deliverables, acceptance, direction, availability, tools, substitution, subcontracting, records, and change control.
- Verify applicable invoicing, tax, registration, social-protection, withholding, expense, and payment treatment with qualified legal and accounting advisers; no allocation is asserted here.
- Draft and review term, termination, confidentiality, IP, warranties, liability, remedies, data handling, notices, governing law, dispute process, execution, and language provisions.
- Verify current legal texts, amendment history, and any status or commencement of Bill 032.26 before relying on employment-law propositions.

## Required language review

EN/FR/AR files are working translations, not reviewed equivalents. A qualified reviewer must verify legal meaning parity, Moroccan terminology, Arabic RTL behavior, numbering, punctuation, and any language-priority decision. Structural matching does not establish semantic parity.

## Release gate

The package is DRAFT, legal review is pending, all language reviews are pending, and generator use is disabled. A review packet is preparation only. Any later legal-review record must identify an authorized human reviewer, the exact version, and content digest. Do not present this package as ready to sign or use.
